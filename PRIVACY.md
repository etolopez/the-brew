# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

The Brew is an offline coffee companion for home brewers. This policy describes the information the current app stores, the choices users have, and what happens when a recipe is shared.

## Information stored locally

The app stores the user's display name, brewing setup, coffees, recipes, brew history, ratings, notes, and preferences on the user's device using local app storage.

The current app:

- Does not require an online account
- Does not send this information to a The Brew server
- Does not include advertising or behavioral tracking
- Does not provide cloud synchronization

## Recipe sharing

When a user chooses to share a recipe, The Brew encodes the selected recipe data into an offline QR code. The user can separately choose whether to include the coffee used with the recipe.

The QR code includes the sender's display name. Anyone who scans the code can read and import the information encoded in it. Users should share QR codes only with people they trust.

## Camera access

Camera access is used only to scan The Brew recipe QR codes. The Brew does not use the camera to identify people, collect photos, or record video.

## Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until the user edits or deletes it, clears the app's stored data, or uninstalls the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data may permanently remove locally stored information.

## User choices

Users can:

- Edit or delete coffees and recipes
- Change their display name and equipment setup
- Choose whether shared recipes include coffee information
- Deny or revoke camera access through device settings
- Clear local app data or uninstall the app

## Security

Local information is protected by the security controls of the user's device and operating system. Users should not place passwords, financial details, medical information, or other sensitive data in coffee names, recipe titles, or tasting notes.

## Changes to this policy

This policy should be updated if The Brew adds online accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, remote APIs, or another feature that changes how information is collected or shared.

## Questions

For privacy questions or requests about this app, contact the project owner or maintainer listed on this repository.
