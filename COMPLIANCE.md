# Compliance and Responsible Use

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

This page summarizes the safeguards currently built into The Brew. It is product information, not a certification or legal advice.

## Scope

The Brew is designed as an offline, personal coffee companion. It currently has no sign-in system, server-side user database, advertising profile, or cloud synchronization feature.

## Data minimization

The app requests information needed for its coffee, recipe, timer, tasting, and sharing features. Optional coffee details, tasting notes, and brew history stay on the device unless the user deliberately encodes selected data into a QR code.

## Permissions

The camera permission is requested only when a user chooses to scan a recipe QR code. Users can deny or revoke camera access and continue using the app's non-scanning features.

## User control

Users control:

- What coffee, recipe, and tasting information they enter
- What information they edit or delete
- Whether camera access is granted
- Which recipes are shared
- Whether a shared recipe includes its associated coffee

## Security

Local data protection depends on the user's device and operating-system security. QR sharing is proximity-based and user initiated. Users should review what they are sharing and avoid entering sensitive personal information.

## Accessibility

The app uses native text, controls, scrolling, and system permission flows. Device font-size and assistive-technology settings may affect presentation. Accessibility issues should be reported to the project owner or maintainer.

## Limitations

The Brew does not claim compliance with a specific legal framework or industry certification. Project owners should review this page and the Privacy Policy with qualified counsel before using or distributing the app in a regulated context.

This document should be reviewed whenever the app adds accounts, remote storage, analytics, advertising, payments, location data, or other networked services.
